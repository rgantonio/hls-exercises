# BAM3 R1: the `bambu` case on Bambu 2026.11, both tiers, 2026-11-05

BAM3 R1 (docs/BAMBU_PLAN.md section 8, "BAM3 outcome and decision"): the `bambu` case moves from Bambu 2024.10 to 2026.11. Bambu 2026.11 was rebuilt for a generic x86-64 target, then `bench/bambu.py` reran both tiers (48 builds, 95 points, 190 simulations in Questa 2022.4), replacing the 2024.10 Verilog in `rtl/<tier>/<kernel>/bambu_w*/`, `results_staging/bambu_cycles.csv` and both reports. The five elementwise kernels' output beats are initialized in their sources since 542fa32 (fault 1 of probe B); fault 2, a 2026.11 bug on beats wider than 24 lanes, is recorded, not worked around. Code at 542fa32.

## Rebuild of Bambu 2026.11 (generic x86-64)

```bash
pixi run bash bench/tools/build_bambu_2611.sh bundle    # sources already in tools/src/panda-2026.11
pixi run bash bench/tools/build_bambu_2611.sh install
bench/tools/in_bambu.sh bambu-2026.11 --version
```

- Flags: the rendered `tools/src/build-2026.11/appimage-compat-cache.cmake` ends with `set(PANDA_OPTIMIZED_FLAGS "-Ofast -march=x86-64-v2 -mtune=generic" CACHE STRING "" FORCE)`, and the in-container build's `CMakeCache.txt` has `PANDA_OPTIMIZED_FLAGS:STRING=-Ofast -march=x86-64-v2 -mtune=generic`.
- The build log (`tools/src/build-2026.11.log`) has no compile command lines (ninja prints `[n/N] Building ...` only), so the flags were checked in the in-container build's `compile_commands.json`: 350 of 569 compile commands have `-march=x86-64-v2`, none has `-march=native`. The other 219 (vendored libraries under `ext/`, `etc/` such as libbambu, two `panda_unit` tests and one `src` file) carry no `-march` at all. `build.ninja` has 350 `-march=x86-64-v2` and no `native`.
- Time: 667 s for the container step (configure, 2122 ninja steps, install, GLIBC audit, appimagetool), with the ccache of the first build; the first, native build took 743 s from an empty ccache.
- Result: `tools/bambu-2026.11/bambu-2026.11.AppImage`, 361649344 bytes, sha256 `e08aaef4821576425855f209c9d880e713b8562beddacc8978f65e0b8cb1659a`, GLIBC audit at most GLIBC_2.17; extracted 1.1 GB. `--version`: `PandA 2026.11 - Revision cda4db2e724df357f749c0c41b12bdc065462ced-HEAD`.
- The fault 2 reproducer (`notes/repro_bam3/`, committed in this round) gives the same result on the generic build as on the native one: 28 lanes, `c = 0`, lane 26's low word 0x000000a8 instead of 0x000012ba; 2024.10 passes; 2026.11 at 8 lanes passes.

The dry run prints no totals; counted from its commands: 24 builds in `done` and 24 in `untiered`, every one `in_bambu.sh bambu-2026.11`, and 190 simulations (95 points, each a no-stall and a stall run: 8 kernels x 6 W x 2 sizes, less rowsum's m4_n16 at W = 32). The round expected 188; 190 is what 95 points give.

The run took 57 s. 85 of 95 points ok, every one of them PASS in the stall run too. The 10 failed points are exactly the expected ones: vecadd, vecmul, matadd, relu and axpy at W = 32, both sizes, `failed: sim` (the stall runs fail as well). No other failure. Every row is `PandA 2026.11 rev cda4db2e` and portable `yes`.

## Step 7: the W = 32 failures are fault 2

The testbench prints only the first three mismatches, so the 10 failing no-stall runs were rerun with their recorded commands and a copy of `tb/` whose print limit is raised (`build/diag_words/tb/`, `bad < 1000000`; `tb/` unchanged). Every mismatch is a low 32-bit word of lanes 26 to 31, the fault 2 pattern of probe B (L26 lo wrong but not 0, the others 0; a zeroed word shows only where the expected word is nonzero, so relu's counts are lower):

| kernel | size | lanes wrong | wrong words (times seen) |
|---|---|---|---|
| vecadd | n64 | 10 of 64 | L26 lo, not 0 (2); L27 to L30 lo (2 each) |
| vecadd | n512 | 79 of 512 | L26 lo, not 0 (15); L27 to L30 lo (16 each) |
| vecmul | n64 | 10 of 64 | L26 lo, not 0 (2); L27 to L30 lo (2 each) |
| vecmul | n512 | 80 of 512 | L26 lo (1, and 15 not 0); L27 to L30 lo (16 each) |
| matadd | m8_n16 | 20 of 128 | L26 lo, not 0 (4); L27 to L30 lo (4 each) |
| matadd | m16_n32 | 79 of 512 | L26 lo, not 0 (15); L27 to L30 lo (16 each) |
| relu | n64 | 9 of 64 | L26 lo, not 0 (2); L27 lo (1); L28 to L30 lo (2 each) |
| relu | n512 | 50 of 512 | L26 lo, not 0 (7); L27 lo (10); L28 lo (8); L29 lo (10); L30 lo (9); L31 lo (6) |

No high word and no lane below 26 is wrong, so fault 1 (lane 0 high, lane 1 low) is gone with the initialized sources. All 10 rows' `fail_reason` in `bambu_cycles.csv` got `; Bambu 2026.11 bug, wide computed beat (notes/repro_bam3)` appended (the CSV rewritten with the same `csv.DictWriter` columns; only those 10 fields changed), then `bambu.py report` rewrote the reports. The reports show a failed point's status only, not its reason.

## Step 8: loop pipelining

Every build pipelined its BEAT loop (BB2): `BB2 II=1 lat=2` or `BB2 II=1 lat=3` everywhere except dot at W = 4 and W = 8, `BB2 II=2 lat=3` (probe B did not build W = 4). Bambu's lines for those two and their neighbours:

| dot | minII | maxII | II line | iteration latency | minimum slack (ns) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| W = 2 | 1 | 2 | `II=1 solution found for loop (BB2)` | 2 | 4.31 |
| W = 4 | 1 | 3 | `II=2 solution found for loop (BB2)` | 3 | 0.0048 |
| W = 8 | 1 | 3 | `II=2 solution found for loop (BB2)` | 3 | 0.070 |
| W = 16 | 1 | 3 | `II=1 solution found for loop (BB2)` | 3 | 0.0048 |

Bambu prints no reason for II = 2. It is not a resource limit: each build has one multiplier per lane (dot W = 4: 4 `ui_mul_node_FU`).

## Questa warnings

All 190 simulations end `Errors: 0, Warnings: 2`. The two warnings are always the same pair, from the 2026.11 top's new `idle_port` output, which our testbenches leave unconnected: `(vopt-2685) [TFMPC] - Too few port connections for 'dut'. Expected <n>, found <n-1>.` and `(vopt-2718) [TFMPC] - Missing connection for port 'idle_port'.` (2024.10's pair was for `out1`.)

## rtl/

144 tracked files changed (54792 lines in, 103139 out: 2026.11's Verilog is shorter) and 48 new `panda_libtech.v`, one per folder: 2026.11 writes its library modules there, and `filelist.f` lists it before `<kernel>.v`. The top is now `p_<mangled name>` (`# top:` in `filelist.f`); `cmd.txt` records `# loop pipelining: ...` and the `bambu-2026.11` command. No file was removed.

## Unexpected

- dot is at II = 2 at W = 4 as well as W = 8 (probe B saw W = 8 only); W = 1, 2, 16 and 32 are II = 1.
- The dry run has 190 simulations, not 188.
- The build log cannot show compile flags (ninja's short output); `compile_commands.json` was used instead.
