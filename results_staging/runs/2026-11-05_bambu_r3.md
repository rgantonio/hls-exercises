# Bambu RTL-only flow, tier untiered (BAM2 R3), 2026-11-05

The whole tier untiered with `bench/bambu.py`, the first time these kernels have been built with Bambu: matadd, relu, axpy and rowsum, W in {1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32}, two sizes each, case `bambu` (Bambu's default setup plus `#pragma HLS unroll` on LANE), 10 ns, nangate45. That is 47 points: rowsum W = 32 at m4_n16 is left out by its valid rule. Bambu builds each kernel and W once, at the larger size, in the podman container (localhost/hls-exercises-bambu:4), without `--simulate`: 24 builds. Our SystemVerilog testbenches (tb/matadd_tb.sv, relu_tb.sv, axpy_tb.sv, rowsum_tb.sv) then simulate each build in Questa 2022.4 on the host at both sizes, each with a no-stall run (cycles) and a random-stall, two-task run (pass/fail): 94 simulations. This is the first time these four testbenches have run against Bambu RTL. Code at 3aac8c8. The build tree was wiped (`rm -rf build`) before the run. Started 2026-11-05 08:06:08.

## Commands


## Results

All 24 builds ok, all 94 simulations PASS, 47 of 47 points ok. No testbench problem showed up. All rows are portable. Bambu PandA 2024.10 rev c2ba6936.

Against the expectations:

- matadd matches vecadd per beat at every W (1.49 to 1.50 at W = 1, about 3 above it). At equal element counts the cycles are the same: m16_n32 equals vecadd n512 at every W.
- relu and axpy match vecadd and vecmul exactly at W ≥ 2. At W = 1 they run at 2.00 cycles per beat (15 FSM states), not 1.5 (see step 7 below).
- axpy is not slower than vecmul: the extra add per lane costs no cycles at any W. axpy and relu give identical no-stall cycles at every point.
- rowsum is vsum plus M − 1 cycles at every W: m4_n16 is vsum n64 + 3 (163, 99, 51, 27, 19 against 160, 96, 48, 24, 16), and m8_n64 is vsum n512 + 7 (1287, 775, 391, 199, 135, 71 against 1280, 768, 384, 192, 128, 64). So it costs one extra cycle per row boundary. Per beat this shows most at small sizes and wide W (4.75 at m4_n16 W = 16).
- cycles_done is cycles + 2 everywhere except matadd at W = 1 (cycles + 3), the same pattern as vecadd and vecmul in tier done.

## Side look: vecadd W = 1 (21 FSM states, about 1.5 cycles per beat)

From `rtl/done/vecadd/bambu_w1/vecadd.v`, read but not changed. There is one read module instance per stream (`A_` and `B_bambu_artificial_ParmMgr_Read_axis_modgen`, one `C_..._Write_axis_modgen`), not two. Each instance's `start_port` is an OR of 5 state selectors (`or_or_start_port0/1/2`), so the controller starts each stream from 5 FSM states:

- Main loop, states S_6 to S_16: A and B are read in S_6, S_8, S_10 and S_12; C is written in S_8, S_10, S_12 and S_14; S_16 branches back to S_6 or on to S_5. That is four beats of each stream per pass, so the loop is unrolled 4×, not 2×, with reads and writes overlapping by one state. The odd states S_7 to S_15 are wait states taken only when a handshake is not ready. Without stalls a pass is 6 states for 4 beats = 1.5 cycles per beat: 16 passes × 6 = 96 at n64 (measured 95), 128 × 6 = 768 at n512 (measured 767).
- Remainder loop, states S_0 to S_4: one beat per pass (read in S_0, write in S_2), entered through S_5 after the main loop. It is for loop_count mod 4 and does not run at n64 or n512.
- 21 states = idle S_17 + 6 main + 5 main-wait + 3 remainder + 2 remainder-wait + S_5 + 3 exit states (S_18, S_19, S_20).

How sure: high on the structure. I read the selector-to-state map and the transitions from the controller, and the pass length matches the measured cycles at both sizes. I did not look at waveforms. Unrolled main loop plus remainder loop is the shape of LLVM's runtime loop unrolling, so the 4× probably comes from Bambu's clang front end and not from our pragma, but I have not checked that. matadd W = 1 has the same structure (5 start states per stream, 21 states). relu and axpy W = 1 have 3 start states per stream (15 FSM states), consistent with a 2× main loop plus the same remainder loop, and with their 2.00 cycles per beat (32 passes of 4 states = 128 at n64, measured 127). At W = 2, vecadd has no OR on the start ports: one start state per stream.

## Unexpected

- relu and axpy at W = 1 run at 2.00 cycles per beat (15 states), not the 1.5 of vecadd, vecmul and matadd (21 states). See the side look above.
- rowsum's cost over vsum is exactly one cycle per row boundary (M − 1), at every W.
- No slow build this time (max 12.9 s). In R1, vecmul W = 16 and vecadd W = 32 took about 39 s against 11 to 15 s for the rest. That points to run-to-run variance or contention, not those kernels, but I have not checked.
- `bambu.py run --tier untiered` also rewrote `results_staging/report_bambu_done.md`, byte-identical to the committed one.
- Untracked `rtl/done/vecadd/opt_w8_c10/` and `rtl/done/vecmul/opt_w8_c10/` are left untouched and not committed.
